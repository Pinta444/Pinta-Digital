This Cookie Policy explains how Pinta Digital ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on pintadigital.com (the "Site"). Please read it together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to tell site owners how their site is used. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, web beacons, local storage and software development kits. In this policy we refer to all of these as "Cookies".

How we use Cookies

We may use the following types of Cookies on the Site:

TypePurposeConsent
Strictly necessaryNeeded for the Site to work, for example to keep it secure, remember your cookie choices, and process forms and payments.Not required

Information collected through Cookies may include device information, browser information, information about your network connection, your IP address, and information about how you interact with the Site.

First-party and third-party Cookies

We set some Cookies ourselves (first-party Cookies). Other Cookies are set by third parties whose services we use, such as analytics, advertising, payment and hosting providers (third-party Cookies). Those third parties use their Cookies under their own privacy notices, and we do not control them. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Legal basis

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), we only set Cookies that are not strictly necessary after you have given your consent. Where we use Cookies on the basis of our legitimate interest, such as keeping the Site secure, the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as described below.

How to manage Cookies

  • Browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete Cookies. How to do this depends on your browser, so check its help pages. If you block all Cookies, parts of the Site may not work properly.
  • Global Privacy Control. If you visit the Site with the Global Privacy Control opt-out preference signal enabled, depending on where you are, we will treat it as a request to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information, and of targeted advertising, for that browser and device.
  • Advertising choices. You can opt out of interest-based advertising from many providers through tools such as Your Online Choices (Europe) or the Digital Advertising Alliance (US).
  • Contact us. You can also ask us to change your choices by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example when we change the Cookies we use or for operational, legal or regulatory reasons. We will post the revised policy on the Site and update the "Last updated" date.
